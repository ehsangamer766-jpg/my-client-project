About Me

Hey, I'm Aarav

I started building websites at 15 after teaching myself HTML and CSS from free tutorials. Since then I've picked up JavaScript, worked with real clients, and learned that good design is really about making things easy to use — not about looking flashy.

I currently take on small freelance projects: portfolio sites, landing pages, and simple business websites. I care about clean code, fast load times, and layouts that work just as well on a phone as they do on a laptop.

When I'm not coding, I'm usually learning something new — right now that's backend development and a bit of design theory.
